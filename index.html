PANACEA

Shift tools in one place.

  • HxHPI builder

    Pick a chief complaint, mark pertinent positives and negatives, get the HPI.
  • PxExam builder

    Focused exams by complaint, plus ICU, full neuro and NIHSS, written for the note.
  • TracingEKG dictation

    Walk Dubin’s five steps with example strips, and get the read and impression.
  • SilhouetteChest X-ray read

    Film check, then A to E finding by finding, with diagrams; get the report and impression.
  • DispoED MDM builder

    Flow-chart inputs that write the MDM and discharge instructions.
  • ConsultConsult call prep

    Pick a service, gather what they’ll ask about, and get a call script and note line.
  • SpectrumID consults

    ID HPI, cultures and antibiotic days, the daily note, and a bugs and drugs reference.
  • AdmitICU & floor H&P

    Problems with ready plans, antibiotics by source, FAST HUGS BID, then send to ICU Rounder.
  • ICU RounderDaily rounds

    Patient profiles through discharge, daily inputs, problem-based A/P.
  • SweepICU considerations

    A morning pass through every system, with open items flagged for rounds.
  • EtherAnesthesia plan

    Review the chart, score the risk, build the anesthetic plan, and print a card for the OR.
  • PrimerICU curriculum

    One lesson a day: readings, what to order, the evidence, and a self-check.
  • CoreEM curriculum

    All of Tintinalli’s, one lesson a day: chapters, workup, treatment and dispo.
  • RhythmEKG curriculum

    Dubin’s Rapid Interpretation of EKG’s, one lesson a day, with your copy open beside it.
  • Rhythm 2ECG curriculum

    Chizner’s ECG Interpretation Made Ridiculously Simple, one lesson a day, ending with 55 practice ECGs.
  • SystoleCardiology curriculum

    Chizner’s Clinical Cardiology Made Ridiculously Simple, one lesson a day, for the cardiology sub-I.
  • NenyaEM rank list

    Weight what matters, add programs as you interview, watch the list sort.
